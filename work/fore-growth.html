Fore Coffee · Associate Product Manager · Aug 2025 to now

Getting new users to stay, for less.

I work on growth and retention in the Fore Coffee app. The question I keep coming back to is whether new users order again, and what it cost to get them.

Role
Associate Product Manager
Area
Consumer app
Focus
Retention, acquisition cost, user quality

New-user vouchers

The problem. The new-user offer was built around the first order.

What we did. With the promo team, I reworked the offer so it also rewards coming back. We watched promo cost and first-order volume next to retention, so a better number couldn’t come from just spending more.

Result. Weekly retention went up and promo cost efficiency improved. Happy to walk through how we decided.

Cheaper sign-in

The problem. Every one-time password we send at sign-in costs money.

What we did. We changed the sign-in and onboarding flow to bring that cost down. We tracked acquisition quality next to cost, so the savings couldn’t come from losing good users.

Result. Sign-in cost went down and acquisition quality held.

TikTok Dining Go

The problem. Offers bought on TikTok needed a way to be redeemed at our cashiers.

What I did. I drove the integration of TikTok Dining Go with our POS and voucher redemption. Each voucher had to redeem once, cleanly, without slowing down the store queue.

Result. It’s live in stores, and it became a real offline sales channel in its first two weeks.

What I learned

A sign-up chart can look great while the business loses money. Before I call anything a win, I check who stayed and what they cost.
