Fore Coffee · In progress

What should the app say while your coffee is being made?

Waiting is part of buying coffee. I’m working on what the app promises about ready time, and how that promise holds up after you order.

Role
Product discovery and requirements
With
Design, Engineering, BI
Status
In progress, not launched

The problem

What should a customer see while the order is being made, so the time feels honest even when the store is packed?

What I’m looking into

  • The gap between what our systems record and what a customer sees on screen.
  • How pickup and delivery customers think about waiting.
  • How to say a store is busy without making the wait feel longer.

My part

Discovery, product framing, requirements and rollout planning. The BI team builds the prediction model.

Where it stands

It hasn’t launched, so there are no results yet. Good accuracy in testing doesn’t prove customers will trust the number, so we’ll test it carefully before a wide rollout.

What I’ve learned so far

Screenshots and backend events can’t show what a customer went through, so I check the real moment on the real screen.
