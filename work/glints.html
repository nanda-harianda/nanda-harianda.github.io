Fewer steps from opening the app to applying.

At Glints I was an APM on the candidate platform. The goal was simple to say and hard to prove: get job seekers to a relevant application with less in the way.

Role
Associate Product Manager
Area
Candidate platform
Focus
Onboarding and apply flow

The problem

Onboarding asked questions candidates didn’t need to answer before seeing jobs. Every extra screen between opening the app and applying was another place to lose someone.

What changed

  • Shorter onboarding. We cut the questions that didn’t help candidates find a job.
  • Suggested job roles. Candidates picked a direction during onboarding, so their first search had a starting point.
  • One-Tap Apply. Candidates could apply straight from the For You feed.

Results

90%onboarding completion rate
+8.6%1-day open-to-apply rate
+32%applications per applicant

Each number was measured on the candidate platform after its change went live. Happy to walk through the setup and baselines in a chat.

My part

I was the PM on these changes. I defined the problem, wrote the requirements and worked with design, engineering and data to ship and measure them.

What I learned

Removing a step often helped more than adding a feature. The real work was proving which step was costing us applicants.
