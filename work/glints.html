Glints · Associate Product Manager · Jun 2024 to Jul 2025

Fewer steps from opening the app to applying.

I was the APM on Glints’ candidate platform. My job was to get job seekers to a relevant application with fewer screens in the way.

Role
Associate Product Manager
Area
Candidate platform
Focus
Onboarding and apply flow

The problem

Onboarding asked questions candidates didn’t need to answer before seeing jobs. Every extra screen between opening the app and applying was one more place to lose someone.

What we changed

  • Shorter onboarding. We cut the questions that didn’t help candidates find a job.
  • Suggested job roles. Candidates picked a direction during onboarding, so their first search had a starting point.
  • One-Tap Apply. Candidates could apply straight from the For You feed.

Results

90%onboarding completion rate
+8.6%1-day open-to-apply rate
+32%applications per applicant

Figures from my CV. Happy to share more context about the work and my role.

My part

I was the PM on these changes. I defined the problem, wrote the requirements and worked with design, engineering and data to ship and measure them.

What I learned

Taking a step out often helped more than adding a feature. The hard part was showing which step was costing us applicants.
